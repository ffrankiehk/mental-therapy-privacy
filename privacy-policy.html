Privacy Policy for Mental Therapy

Last updated: December 30, 2025

Introduction

Mental Therapy ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Mental Therapy mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

Information Collection and Use

Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information from users of our App.

Usage Data

The App may collect basic usage statistics to improve user experience, but this data is anonymous and cannot be used to identify individual users.

VR Content

All VR therapy sessions and meditation content are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or transmit your usage patterns or session data to our servers.

Data Storage

All user data, including:

  • App preferences
  • Session history
  • Viewing progress

is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

Our App may use third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies:

  • Google Play Services (for app distribution)
  • Expo (for app framework)

Children's Privacy

Our App does not specifically target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Health Disclaimer

Important: This App provides VR-based relaxation and meditation experiences for mental wellness. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have serious mental health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

所有 VR 治療課程和冥想內容都儲存在您的裝置本地。我們不會上傳或傳輸您的使用模式或課程資料到我們的伺服器。
